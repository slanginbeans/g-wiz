Remove background

An AI model built into G-WIZ finds the main subject (a person, an animal, an object) and the cut then follows the picture's real edges, keeping soft hair and fur. It runs on this device and works offline. Mark the subject or the background with the hint brushes when it misses a part.

Learns the background colors from the border, works out what the subject looks like, and cuts along the picture's real edges. Often the more exact choice for logos, drawings and shapes on a plain background. Mark the subject or the background with the hint brushes when it guesses wrong.

Reads the colors along the image border, then erases that background inward until it reaches the subject's edges.

Hints

Paint over parts that were cut wrongly. Green marks the subject, magenta marks background. Right button paints the other color, Alt erases a hint.
